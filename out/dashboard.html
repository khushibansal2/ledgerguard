How work is routed

Each layer costs more and is trusted less than the one above it. 48% of events are settled by arithmetic that cannot hallucinate; only what genuinely fails to tie out reaches a model, and even then the model may only choose which explanation to test — a deterministic tool computes the answer and a policy gate decides whether it may be booked.

What it still gets wrong

Measured over 9,400 settlement events, stated plainly.

  • 69 unresolved (timing lag cross month (69)). All one shape: an unequal cohort of identical amounts — three indistinguishable bills against two indistinguishable payments. No fact separates them, so the engine refuses instead of guessing, and each one appears on the exception ledger above.
  • 6 partial matches. Two short-payments from one vendor, each net of a credit note, where the resolver applied the sibling's credit note. The pair nets correctly; the individual application does not.
  • The policy gate is unproven on this data. Removing it raises straight-through from 93.5% to 99.2% with no measured loss of precision. It is retained as tail-risk insurance against errors this synthetic distribution does not contain — a judgement call, not a result the numbers here support.
  • Synthetic data is not production data. The FX curve, fee schedules and tax rates are frozen tables. Real books add partial settlements, reversals, chargebacks and multi-currency netting that this build does not yet model.
